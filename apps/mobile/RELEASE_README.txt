Capri Mobile 0.4.5 beta
=======================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  Capri-Mobile-0.4.5-beta.apk          the app: open it on your phone (allow installing unknown apps if asked)
  Capri Mobile 0.4.5 beta Source.zip   the source code
  README.txt                           this file

Needs Android 7 or newer. Installs over older versions: your settings, memories and brains stay.

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.4.5
  Continuous talk
    - Setup > Her behaviour > Continuous talk. Tap her once and just talk: she keeps listening and
      answering until you tap her again. Hold her to type instead.

  Go mini by voice or chat
    - "Capri, go mini", "can you turn mini", "make yourself small": she shrinks into her mini form.
    - "Come back to full size", "go big": she's back.

  Timers and alarms
    - "Set a timer for 5 minutes", "remind me in 20 minutes", "wake me up at 7:30", "alarm at 6 pm".
      She sets them in your phone's clock app, so they ring even if she's closed. Works in all 8 languages.

  Fixes
    - Russian "look at this" and "go to sleep" now work.

ALSO IN 0.4.4
  Brains: RAM and a recommendation
    - Every brain in the Brain tab says how much RAM it needs and how much it runs best with.
    - The best brain for your phone gets a "Recommended for this phone" badge.
    - Too little RAM for a brain on the phone? She points you to NVIDIA's free cloud brain.

  Miku skin
    - Setup > Her look > Miku.
    - Her own art, standing about as tall as Capri. Held up, she uses her grab pose.
    - Her own mini eyes. When she sleeps she goes mini, eyes shut.
    - Her speech bubble is blue, with her own face in it.

  Peeking from the side
    - Fling Capri (or the Watcher) hard into the side of the screen and she peeks out with her new
      peek art: mirrored on the left, blinking, and talking while she peeks.

  Fixes
    - Fixed a crash after talking to her with her Qwen ears, when she had been restarted in between.

ALSO IN 0.4.2 / 0.4.3
  - 8 languages: English, Espanol, Portugues, Francais, Russian, Italiano, Deutsch, Polski.
    Pick one the first time you open the app, or change it any time in Setup.
  - The whole app, her lines and her replies switch to it.
